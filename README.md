### 🛡️ MARFL — Robust Federated Aggregation

[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/View_repository-181717?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Erick0330/MARFL)

Undergraduate thesis. A multi-aggregation operator that defends federated training against **model-poisoning attacks**, composing robust aggregators along a **temporal axis** (Multi-Krum → phase transition at round 13) and a **spatial axis** (Krum-exclude → Trimmed Mean pipeline).

Benchmarked against **5 aggregators** under **6 attacks** — sustains **87%** accuracy where the strongest single-operator baseline drops to **61%**.

`PyTorch` · `FLEX/FLEXClash` · `Adversarial ML` · `DOE`
